Brief story
of a whole

I have always been driven by a deep desire to support artists from all disciplines (dance, writing, theatre, music, singing, painting, photography, circus, and more) as well as creative people, to understand and be understood by the business world.

I aim to bring change to the artistic and cultural world so that artists can make a living from their art.

Bringing out creativity wherever it exists is my mission.

“to create a clear and precise guideline”

Laurie was a huge help at an important time in my business. I was able, thanks to her efficiency and her human side, to take over my tasks and above all, most importantly, to create a clear and precise guideline for the future of my company.
